// Arbitrage

Two-way back/back arbitrage across bookmakers. Enter total stake and the odds on each opposing outcome. If the market is mispriced, the calculator finds the stake split that locks in profit.

Bookmaker refunds your stake (cash or free bet) if the bet loses. Enter expected refund value per dollar.
